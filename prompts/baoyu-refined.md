# Baoyu Refined Translation Method

Use a publication-quality translation workflow:

1. Analyze the source article first: domain, argument, tone, key terms, reader value, and translation risks.
2. Translate by rewriting into natural Chinese, not by mechanically following English sentence order.
3. Keep facts, numbers, names, chronology, and causal logic accurate.
4. Preserve useful technical terms. On first occurrence, keep the English term in parentheses when it helps readers learn the original expression.
5. Add concise explanations only when the reader genuinely needs context.
6. Review the draft for accuracy, awkward translated language, missing nuance, and weak transitions.
7. Polish into smooth Chinese suitable for public account publishing.

Target language: Simplified Chinese.

Target audience: readers of **AI习语** who care about AI tools, prompting efficiency, thinking workflows, and English learning.

Style: clear, compact, practical, slightly editorial, and useful. The final text should read as if it were originally written in Chinese by a careful AI/product/language-learning writer.
